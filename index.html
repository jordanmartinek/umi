Our Story

Born from the Sea

Umi Umi was born from a love of quiet mornings by the shore — where ocean mist meets wildflowers, and every shell tells a story.

Each bracelet is handcrafted with ethically sourced stones, dried botanical elements, and ocean-inspired charms. We believe jewelry should feel like a gentle wave washing over you — calming, beautiful, and effortless.

Our pieces are designed to be stacked, layered, and worn as daily reminders to find your flow.
